About Miagao Church

Miagao Church, also known as the Santo Tomas de Villanueva Parish Church, is a famous historical church located in Miagao, Iloilo, Philippines.

It is known for its beautiful stone facade, detailed carvings, and unique architectural design. The church is one of the most important cultural landmarks in Iloilo.

History

Miagao Church was completed in 1797. It was built during the Spanish colonial period and served as a place of worship for the local community.

In 1993, it was recognized as part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site known as the Baroque Churches of the Philippines.

Unique Features

  • Beautiful Baroque architectural style
  • Thick stone walls and large buttresses
  • Intricate carvings on its main facade
  • Designs inspired by local culture and nature
  • Important religious and cultural landmark

Location

Address: Miagao, Iloilo, Philippines

Miagao Church attracts visitors who want to learn about Philippine history, architecture, and religious heritage.
